Placeholder text. Your homepage has one job: show visitors they're in the right place, then help them take the next step.

## The first screen

One sentence saying what you do and for whom, then a clear button: call, request a quote, book.

## Your services, in a few lines

A short summary of each service, with a link to its page.

## Proof

Customer reviews, photos of your work, the areas you cover.

## A final call to action

Repeat how to contact you at the bottom of the page, for those who read everything.
