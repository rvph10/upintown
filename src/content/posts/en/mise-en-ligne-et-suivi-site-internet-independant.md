You are a lawyer, architect, accountant, therapist, tradesperson or shopkeeper. Your job is to advise, to care for people, to design, to repair or to sell. Putting a website online and looking after it over time should not be added to that list. UpInTown takes care of putting your site online and looking after it, so you can get on with your work.

In practice, this covers two distinct moments. First comes going live, when everything is set up and the site becomes accessible. Then comes ongoing care, month after month. Knowing what happens at each stage helps you decide with confidence.

## Two moments, two kinds of work

A website does not stop on the day it becomes visible. A site that is built and then left alone ages quickly: opening hours are out of date, a page stays empty, a personal email address is still printed on a business card. UpInTown works on both stages, going live and then ongoing care, so that everything stays consistent.

## Going live

Going live brings together everything that must be ready before the site is open to the public. UpInTown takes on this work, step by step.

### The domain name and hosting

The domain name is the address of your website, most often ending in .be for a self-employed person based in Belgium. Hosting is the place where the site lives and stays available around the clock. UpInTown handles both: the domain is registered and set up, and hosting is put in place. You remain the owner of everything, the domain as well as the content.

### The text and the pages

A readable site relies on clear text. The site is built turnkey, text included, and goes live in 3 to 5 weeks. The pages present your business, your services and your contact details, without needless jargon.

### The professional email address

An address like firstname@yourname.be looks more polished than a personal one. UpInTown sets up this professional email address together with hosting and the domain name. Your messages stay tied to your business.

## Ongoing care

Once the site is online, it keeps living. A new project is added, a price changes, a typo slips into a paragraph. Without care, these details pile up and end up giving a neglected impression. UpInTown's website care covers several areas.

### Small changes

A text to correct, a photo to replace, opening hours to adjust: the request goes through a single contact and the change is made within 2 working days. You do not have to dive into a tool or work out who to call.

### Security and upkeep

An online site has to stay up to date to keep working properly. As part of monthly website care, UpInTown keeps an eye on this, along with hosting, the domain name and the email address, at a fixed price. The aim is simple: your site stays in working order without you having to think about it.

### Local visibility

Beyond the site itself, your local online presence matters. Your Google Business Profile, your reviews, directories such as the Pages d'Or and the pages of your site must all say the same thing. Local search work (being found by people looking nearby) is the steady background job that keeps this information consistent and up to date, month after month, without jargon.

## What stays with you

UpInTown takes care of going live and ongoing care, but a few decisions remain yours. You choose what you want to show, you approve the text and you pass on information as it changes: a new address, a new service, a recent photo. That is all UpInTown expects from you. The rest of your time goes back to your work, your clients and your diary.

## One contact, one fixed price

Many self-employed people juggle one provider for the website, another for email and a third for visibility. UpInTown brings these together under a single point of contact, with a fixed price announced in advance. You know what is covered and what it costs, with no surprises.
