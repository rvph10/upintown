Placeholder text. On a phone, on the move, every second of waiting counts. A slow site makes a business look careless.

## The most common causes

- Heavy images, uploaded straight from the camera.
- Plugins piling up over the years.
- Hosting that's too small for the site.

## What can be done

Compressing images, removing what's no longer used and checking the site every month often makes it noticeably faster.
