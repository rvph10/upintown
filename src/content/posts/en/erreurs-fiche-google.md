Placeholder text. Your Google profile is often the first thing a customer sees when looking for a local business. A few gaps are enough to make it invisible.

## 1. A category that's too vague

Your main category tells Google what you do. Pick the most precise one, then add secondary categories.

## 2. Opening hours that are out of date

A customer who finds the door closed rarely comes back. Remember public holidays and time off.

## 3. No recent photos

Photos of your place, your team or your work build trust before the first contact.

## 4. Reviews left unanswered

Replying to every review, good or bad, shows you're present and listening.

## 5. Different details from one site to another

Your name, address and phone number should match everywhere: website, Google profile and directories.
