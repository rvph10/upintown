Placeholder text. An email address on your own domain shows your business is established. It's a small detail that matters when a customer is choosing between two providers.

## What your customers see

An address like contact@your-business.com reminds people of your website with every message, and it stays the same if you change email provider.

## Switching without losing anything

Your old messages can be imported, and your old address can forward new messages during the transition.

- Let your regular customers know.
- Update your Google profile and social accounts.
- Keep the old address active for a few months.
