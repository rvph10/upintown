Look up the price of local SEO and you will mostly find invitations to request a quote. There is a simple reason for that: the term covers very different jobs, from updating a Google Business Profile to working across a whole website. Before you compare figures, it helps to know what you are buying and what you can do yourself for nothing.

## What exactly is local SEO?

It is everything that helps your business appear when someone looks for your trade nearby, on Google and on Google Maps. Think "electrician Namur" or "physio Wavre".

According to [Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en), local ranking comes down to three things: relevance (does your profile match the search?), distance (how close are you to the person searching?) and prominence (how well known is your business?).

## What costs nothing

Two things are worth knowing before you ask for any quote.

**The Google Business Profile is free.** [Adding or claiming your business costs nothing](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778?hl=en), as long as you are eligible for one. Nobody can sell you the profile itself.

**A better position can't be bought.** Google is clear on this: [there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en). What you pay a provider for is work and time, never a place in the results.

With a little regularity, you can handle the essentials yourself:

- complete your profile: category, opening hours, services, photos
- ask happy customers for a review, and [reply to each one](/en/blog/reply-to-google-reviews)
- check that your name, address and phone number match on your website and in directories

For a straightforward business in an area with few competitors, that may well be enough. The article on [local visibility on Google](/en/blog/local-google-visibility) goes through these steps.

## What you pay a provider for

If you hand this work over, here is what the price usually covers.

| What gets done | How often | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setting up the profile and directory listings | Once | Is there a setup fee? |
| Keeping the profile current, posting | Monthly | How many posts? |
| Replying to reviews | Ongoing | Do you approve the replies? |
| Reviewing searches, adjusting pages | Monthly | Which pages, and how is the result shown? |
| Reporting | Monthly or quarterly | Does it show what was done? |

Published prices are still rare in this field, and they don't always cover the same thing. One Belgian agency that does publish its prices quotes [full SEO from €700 a month, or an audit from €595](https://www.clickforest.com/fr/blog/prix-seo-geo). That is broader work than local SEO alone, and it can make sense for a large site or a fiercely contested market. For a sole trader or small business serving a few towns, the need is often more modest.

So compare the work included before you compare the figures, and look at the total for a year, setup fees included.

## Warning signs

- **A guaranteed top spot.** Nobody can promise one, since Google doesn't sell it.
- **Fake or paid-for reviews.** [Google bans and removes them](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en), including those given in exchange for a discount.
- **A profile that isn't in your name.** It should stay yours if you change provider.
- **No detail on the work done.** A report should tell you in plain words what changed.
- **Dozens of near-identical pages**, with only the town name swapped. They do nothing for your visitors.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. What work is done each month, in practice?
2. Is there a setup fee or a minimum term?
3. Does the Google Business Profile stay in my name?
4. Who replies to reviews, and can I read the replies first?
5. When and how will I find out what has been done?
6. What happens if I stop?
